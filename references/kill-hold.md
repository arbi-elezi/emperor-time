**process**, not a product you ship. This page is the dual-hat note: against
ask-spec out-of-scope (no-gos), the operator (or stranger harness) may emit
only three verbs. Nothing else.

## Three verbs only

When the ask pushes past stamped out-of-scope / no-gos:

| Verb | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **kill** | Stop. Do not ship the scope-greedy ask. |
| **hold** | Park until client or PO widens or cuts. |
| **widen-with-client** | Explicit client/PO ACCEPT before expanding no-gos. |

No fourth verb. Negotiate, soft-interpret, and "just this once" are not on the
list. If you need more scope, you need **widen-with-client**, not clever
wording.
